# A Whirlwind Tour of Rust

## Day 1 &amp; Day 2 &middot; Prof. Kristopher Micinski

<div class="footer">cis352-s26</div>

Note:
Welcome. We are going to spend two lectures on Rust. The goal is not to
make you a Rust expert in two days. The goal is to give you enough
exposure that (a) you understand what makes Rust interesting as a
programming language, (b) you can read Rust code, and (c) you know
where to look when you want to dig deeper.

We will run every non-trivial example in the Rust Playground as we go.
